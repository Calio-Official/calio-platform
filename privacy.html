C.A.L.I.O
Privacy Policy
Overview

C.A.L.I.O (Compliance Analytics Layer for Insurance Operations) is a browser extension that helps you review public U.S. SEC EDGAR filings. Filing contents are processed on your device. We do not operate a filing storage server and we do not sell personal data.

Last updated: 2026-07-10

Data we process
Public filing text: requested from sec.gov, data.sec.gov, and related SEC hosts when you search companies or extract a document. Processed locally in your browser / extension. Not uploaded to C.A.L.I.O servers.
Local preferences: settings such as optional account email, product-update opt-in, mail client choice, sidebar collapse state, and usage counters — stored in Chrome extension storage on your computer.
Extract history & watchlist: stored locally so you can reopen recent extracts and monitor companies you choose. You can clear this data from Preferences or the app sidebar.
Optional email: only if you enter and save an email. Product updates are sent only if you also opt in. You can remove the email and clear local data at any time.
Usage counters: local monthly/hourly extract counts to respect SEC fair-access guidelines and product limits.
What we do not collect
We do not sell your data.
We do not run advertising trackers inside the extension for third-party ads.
We do not require a C.A.L.I.O account for core extract, compare, or export features.
Permissions
storage — preferences, history, watchlist, usage (on your device).
tabs / windows / scripting — open the app and extract on EDGAR pages you visit.
downloads — Excel and PDF/report files you request.
alarms / notifications — optional watchlist monitoring for new filings.
Host access — sec.gov, data.sec.gov, efts.sec.gov for public EDGAR data only.
Third parties

When you load filings, your browser communicates with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) websites under their terms and fair-access guidelines. C.A.L.I.O does not control SEC servers.

Your controls
Clear search history, extract history, and watchlist in the app.
Clear local data from Preferences.
Uninstall the extension to remove extension storage.
Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact published with the Chrome Web Store listing (or the contact in the extension User-Agent string for SEC fair-access identification).

This product summarizes public EDGAR information. It is not investment advice. Confirm material figures on the official SEC filing before use.
