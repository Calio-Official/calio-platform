Origin & Mission

Democratizing Institutional Financial Intelligence.

Public company disclosures are funded by taxpayers and mandated by the United States Congress to belong to every citizen equally. Every 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, and Form 4 published on SEC EDGAR is public domain data.

Yet for over thirty years, Wall Street terminal conglomerates have locked this public data behind prohibitive $25,000 to $35,000 per-year subscription fees. Independent equity researchers, boutique analysts, and university students were left copy-pasting numbers from clunky HTML tables into Excel by hand.

The Birth of CALIO

CALIO was created to solve a singular problem: Why can't your browser parse SEC filings directly, build an interactive DCF valuation model in seconds, and export a clean Excel model without needing a remote cloud server?

By leveraging modern browser memory and client-side JavaScript execution, CALIO executes complex XBRL parsing, peer benchmarking matrices across 25 sectors, and forensic calculations (Beneish M-Score, Altman Z-Score) entirely inside your local machine.

The 3 Core Pillars of CALIO

1. Zero-Cloud Confidentiality

We do not operate a remote filing database. Your confidential ticker lookups, research watchlists, and custom DCF assumptions remain 100% on your local computer.

2. Click-to-Source Auditability

Every extracted number traces back to the exact paragraph and XBRL concept in the official SEC EDGAR filing, ensuring compliance teams and portfolio managers can verify every dollar.

3. Institutional SEC Pacing

Full adherence to SEC Fair Access guidelines. Requests are queued and paced client-side to guarantee zero network strain on government infrastructure.
