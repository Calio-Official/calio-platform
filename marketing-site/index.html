Company desk

Search by ticker or name. Timeline of high-signal filings plus tables for 10-K/Q, 8-K, ownership, and insider forms.

Form-aware extract

Structured fields for financials, 8-K events, Form 3/4/5, 13D/G, and Form 144 — not one generic dump for every form type.

Excel & PDF export

Multi-sheet workbooks for models and print-ready PDF reports for notes or sharing — without retyping from EDGAR HTML.

Built for EDGAR work, not another dashboard fad

Raw EDGAR is free and complete — and slow to turn into a clean sheet. CALIO sits next to SEC.gov so you can extract, compare two periods, keep local history, and watch companies for new filings.

  • Compare periods — side-by-side structure with Excel export for models.
  • Quality badge — know when an extract pack looks incomplete before you trust it.
  • Watchlist — local list with optional new-filing alerts (alarms).
  • Fair access — paced requests and usage limits aligned with SEC guidance.
