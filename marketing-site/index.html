The Origin & Philosophy

Why Legacy Terminals Cost $25,000/Year (And Why Public SEC Data Shouldn't)

Every public company filing belongs to the public domain. Yet for decades, legacy research terminals have charged individual researchers five-figure annual subscriptions simply to reformat and display SEC EDGAR tables.

We engineered CALIO to run 100% in your browser's memory. By parsing raw HTML and XBRL facts locally on your machine, we eliminate cloud server databases, preserve your absolute confidentiality, and provide institutional valuation tools at a fraction of legacy cost.
