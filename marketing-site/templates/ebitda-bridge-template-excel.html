1. The Institutional EBITDA Reconciliation Walk

While companies frequently report customized non-GAAP EBITDA definitions, institutional analysts build standard reconciliation bridges to identify structural cash leakages:

# The Quality of Earnings Walk:
Reported GAAP Operating Income (EBIT)
(+) Depreciation Expense (PP&E physical asset wear)
(+) Amortization Expense (Acquired intangible write-offs)
= Standard EBITDA
(+) Stock-Based Compensation (Non-cash employee grant expense)
(±) One-time Restructuring, Legal & Acquisition Charges
= Adjusted Institutional EBITDA

2. The Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) Trap

In corporate reporting, management teams routinely add back Stock-Based Compensation to non-GAAP metrics, claiming it is a "non-cash" item. However, in private equity and equity research:

  • Dilution Cost: SBC dilutes existing shareholders by expanding share counts over time.
  • Cash Outflow Disguise: If a firm buys back shares to offset dilution, the buyback is a real cash outflow recorded under financing activities (CFF), not operating cash flow.
  • Real FCF Calculation: The template features a dedicated toggle calculating Real FCF = Reported FCF − Stock-Based Compensation.
