1. The Unlevered Free Cash Flow (UFCF) Framework

In institutional corporate finance and equity research, an intrinsic valuation is built on Unlevered Free Cash Flow (Free Cash Flow to Firm). UFCF reflects the cash generated by core operations that remains available to all capital providers (both debt and equity holders) after funding necessary capital expenditures and working capital commitments.

# Mathematical Formulation:
EBIT (Operating Income)
− Cash Taxes [EBIT × (1 − Effective Tax Rate)]
= NOPAT (Net Operating Profit After Tax)
+ Depreciation & Amortization (Non-Cash Addback)
− Capital Expenditures (CapEx)
± Change in Net Working Capital (ΔNWC)
= Unlevered Free Cash Flow (UFCF)

2. Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

To determine the present value of forecasted cash flows, future UFCFs are discounted by the firm's composite opportunity cost of capital. The workbook dynamically models:

  • Cost of Equity ($R_e$): Derived via the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM): $R_e = R_f + \beta \times ERP$, where $R_f$ is the 10-Year US Treasury yield and $ERP$ is the Equity Risk Premium (typically 4.5% to 5.5%).
  • Cost of Debt ($R_d$): Weighted average interest rate on contractual debt obligations, adjusted for corporate tax deductibility: $R_d \times (1 − t)$.
  • Capital Weighting: Market capitalization of equity ($E$) versus total debt book value ($D$).
