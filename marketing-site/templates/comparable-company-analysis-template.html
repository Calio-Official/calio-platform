1. The Enterprise Value (EV) Bridge

To compare companies with disparate debt and cash positions, relative valuation uses Enterprise Value (the total value of core operating assets) rather than raw market capitalization:

# Enterprise Value Equation:
Equity Value (Share Price × Diluted Shares Outstanding)
+ Total Debt (Short-Term Borrowings + Long-Term Debt)
+ Preferred Stock & Non-Controlling (Minority) Interests
− Cash & Cash Equivalents
= Enterprise Value (EV)

2. Valuation Multiples & Capital Structure Neutrality

When selecting valuation multiples, enterprise metrics must pair with operational denominators, while equity metrics pair with post-interest denominators:

Multiple Denominator Capital Structure Neutral? Primary Use Case
EV / EBITDA Operating Cash Flow proxy Yes Standard M&A and leveraged buyout (LBO) benchmark
EV / Revenue Top-line sales Yes High-growth tech, SaaS, or early-stage unprofitable firms
P / E Diluted Net Income No (Impacted by Debt) Mature, dividend-paying cash generators
EV / Free Cash Flow Unlevered FCF Yes Capital-intensive businesses with varying D&A schedules
