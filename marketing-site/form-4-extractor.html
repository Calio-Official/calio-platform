Form 4 extractor for SEC EDGAR

Insider transactions · Form 3 / 4 / 5 · Local Chrome extract

Form 4 reports insider buys, sells, and derivative activity. The HTML can be dense; ownership XML is structured but awkward to skim. A good extractor should pull insider fields — not revenue and EPS from a different pipeline.

What a Form 4 extract should include

  • Issuer and reporting owner
  • Relationship (officer, director, 10% owner)
  • Transaction date and code (e.g. P = open-market purchase)
  • Shares, price, remaining ownership when available
  • Links back to the original EDGAR document

How CALIO handles Form 4

CALIO uses a form-aware pack for insider forms (3/4/5). When you run Open & extract on a Form 4, the product focuses on ownership and transaction structure instead of forcing a generic 10-K financial extract onto the page.

Where helpful, CALIO can use the filing’s structured companion (XML) so fields stay closer to what was actually filed.

Quick workflow

  1. Search the company in CALIO or open the Form 4 on EDGAR.
  2. Run Open & extract.
  3. Confirm the form type badge is Form 4 (not UNKNOWN).
  4. Export Excel if you need a sheet for a watchlist or model.

Common mistakes

  • Treating every Form 4 as a bullish buy — check transaction codes and context.
  • Using delayed third-party sites when you need the actual filed amounts.
  • Expecting real-time trading edge; Form 4s are filed after the trade window rules allow.
