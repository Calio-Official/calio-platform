Compliance & Trust

Privacy Policy & Zero-Cloud Architecture.

Effective Date: 2026-09-01 • Contact: caliointel@gmail.com

CALIO is committed to institutional confidentiality. When conducting investment research, merger analysis, or valuation modeling, your company queries, valuation assumptions, and research watchlists must remain strictly private.

1. 100% On-Device Processing

All SEC EDGAR filing data is fetched directly from government endpoints (sec.gov, data.sec.gov) using secure HTTPS requests originating directly from your browser. The parsing of XBRL tags, 3-statement linking, and DCF calculations occur in your local device memory. CALIO operates zero servers that store, log, or inspect the filings you view.

2. Local Storage & Watchlists

Any saved watchlists, extraction histories, or custom DCF assumptions are persisted exclusively in your browser's local sandbox (chrome.storage.local). This data never traverses the internet to CALIO.

3. No Sale of User Data

We never sell, broker, or monetize your research queries or usage patterns. We believe research confidentiality is non-negotiable for professional finance.
