Methodology & Quality of Earnings

Institutional Rigor Behind Every Ratio

CALIO bypasses third-party scraping aggregators to extract raw GAAP XBRL tags directly from SEC EDGAR filings.

Beneish M-Score Forensics

An 8-variable probabilistic model that uncovers earnings manipulation risk before it surfaces in market repricing. Calculates Days Sales in Receivables (DSRI), Gross Margin Degradation (GMI), Asset Quality (AQI), and Total Accruals to Total Assets (TATA).

Safe Threshold: < -1.78

Altman Z-Score Solvency

A 5-factor multivariate credit risk formula quantifying two-year probability of corporate insolvency. Weighs Working Capital / Total Assets, Retained Earnings, EBIT, Market Value of Equity vs. Total Liabilities, and Asset Turnover.

Safe Zone: > 2.99

FCF Conversion Audit

Measures Free Cash Flow as a percentage of Net Income. Companies consistently converting > 90% of GAAP earnings into hard cash flow demonstrate superior quality of earnings, while sub-50% conversion signals heavy accrual distortions.

Target: > 85% Conversion
