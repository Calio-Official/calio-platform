Frequently Asked Questions (CRM 10-K & Valuation)

How did Salesforce expand GAAP operating margins from 3.3% to nearly 20%?

Spurred by activist investor engagements in 2023, Salesforce dramatically curbed stock-based compensation growth, flattened management layers, disbanded non-core real estate leases, and halted dilutive mega-acquisitions.

What is Agentforce and how does it monetize?

Agentforce is Salesforce's autonomous AI agent platform that handles customer service and sales tasks without human intervention, priced on a consumption model of $2 per conversation plus Data Cloud credit consumption.

What is Salesforce's Remaining Performance Obligation (RPO)?

Current and non-current RPO exceeds $57 billion, representing contracted future subscription revenue that provides multi-year revenue visibility.
