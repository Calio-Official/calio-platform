Frequently Asked Questions (XOM 10-K & Valuation)

How does the $60B Pioneer Natural Resources acquisition impact ExxonMobil's upstream production?

Closing in mid-2024, Pioneer doubled Exxon's Permian Basin footprint to over 1.4 million net acres, expanding Permian production past 1.3 million oil-equivalent barrels per day with breakeven costs below $35/barrel.

What makes Exxon's Guyana offshore development unique in the energy industry?

The Stabroek block in Guyana (where Exxon is operator with 45% interest) holds over 11 billion barrels of oil equivalent, producing over 650,000 bpd with industry-leading FPSO operating costs below $10 per barrel.

How much cash did ExxonMobil return to shareholders in FY2024?

Exxon returned approximately $33 billion to shareholders in FY2024 through $16.3 billion in cash dividends (42 consecutive years of dividend increases) and $16.7 billion in gross share repurchases.
