Frequently Asked Questions (GOOGL 10-K & Valuation)

What is Google Cloud's current operating margin and profitability trajectory?

Google Cloud achieved significant operating leverage in FY2024, expanding operating margins to approximately 11.2% (generating over $4.8 billion in annual segment operating income). This turnaround from historic losses is driven by enterprise GenAI infrastructure demand, TPU v5e/v5p cluster deployments, and Google Workspace seat expansion.

How does Alphabet's Traffic Acquisition Cost (TAC) affect gross margin?

TAC paid to distribution partners (such as Apple for default Safari search placement) and network members accounts for approximately 15% of Google advertising revenues ($53 billion annually). Even with rising distribution costs, gross margin expanded 60 bps to 57.3% in FY2024 due to direct search volume growth and YouTube subscription mix.

How much does Alphabet spend on capital expenditures for AI data centers?

Alphabet invested $52.5 billion in CapEx in FY2024 (up 62.8% YoY), primarily allocated to technical infrastructure including custom Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), GPU cluster servers, and submarine fiber optic networking to support Gemini foundation models and Google Cloud workloads.
