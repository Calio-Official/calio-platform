Frequently Asked Questions (NVIDIA 10-K & Valuation)

How much of NVIDIA's total revenue is driven by the Data Center segment?

NVIDIA's Data Center business (reported under the Compute & Networking segment) accounts for approximately 85% of total corporate revenues, generating over $107 billion annually. This growth is driven by demand for accelerated computing architectures including Hopper H100/H200 and Blackwell B200 GPUs across cloud service providers, sovereign AI nations, and enterprise hyperscalers.

What is NVIDIA's Free Cash Flow conversion rate from GAAP Net Income?

NVIDIA demonstrates exceptional cash conversion, with Free Cash Flow converting at over 96% of reported GAAP Net Income. In the latest annual period, NVIDIA generated $76.50 billion in Operating Cash Flow and $70.30 billion in Free Cash Flow against $72.88 billion in Net Income, confirming pristine earnings quality with zero reliance on aggressive accrual accounting.

How do wafer supply agreements with TSMC impact NVIDIA's gross margins?

As a fabless semiconductor designer, NVIDIA relies on Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) for advanced packaging (CoWoS) and leading-edge process nodes (4N/3nm). Despite rising foundry wafer prices, NVIDIA's gross margin has expanded from 56.9% in FY2023 to 75.0% in FY2025 due to proprietary software moats (CUDA), networking hardware integration (Quantum InfiniBand), and enterprise software licenses.
