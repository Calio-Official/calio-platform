3. Forensic Footnote Analysis: Customer Concentration, Inventory & Capital Allocation

Audited Balance Sheet & Footnotes • FY2026 10-K

Beyond headline revenue growth, an institutional forensic inspection of NVIDIA's FY2026 Form 10-K footnotes reveals four structural balance sheet and working-capital shifts:

Note 16 Customer Concentration
36% Revenue From 2 Buyers ($77.7B)

In FY2026, Customer A accounted for 22% ($47.51B) and Customer B accounted for 14% ($30.23B) of total consolidated revenue — up sharply from 12% and 11% in FY2025.

Inventory & Write-Downs (Blackwell Ramp)
$21.40B Inventory (+112.3% YoY)

Net inventory doubled from $10,080M to $21,403M (Work-in-Process: $8,822M; Finished Goods: $8,774M), while inventory write-downs (InventoryWriteDown) rose 2.5x from $1,600M to $4,000M, compressing Gross Margin by -390 bps to 71.1%.

Working Capital & Receivables
$38.47B A/R (DSO: 65.0 Days)

Accounts receivable increased by +$15,399M (+66.8% YoY) from $23,065M to $38,466M, while Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO) expanded to 125.0 days ($21,403M inventory against $62,475M COGS).

Capital Allocation & Fortress Liquidity
$40.09B Buybacks vs. $6.39B SBC

NVIDIA spent $40,086M repurchasing 282M common shares (6.3x its $6,387M stock-based compensation), lowering diluted shares by -1.2% YoY to 24,514M while holding $42,656M in net cash and $58.5B in remaining buyback authorization.

Frequently Asked Questions (NVIDIA FY2026 10-K & Valuation)

How much of NVIDIA's total revenue is driven by the Data Center segment in FY2026?

Per Note 16 of NVIDIA's FY2026 Form 10-K (for the fiscal year ended January 25, 2026), Data Center revenue reached $193,737 million ($193.74 billion), representing 89.7% of total consolidated revenue ($215,938 million). Within Data Center, Compute generated $162,361 million (+58.9% YoY) and Networking surged +141.5% YoY to $31,376 million.

What is NVIDIA's Free Cash Flow conversion rate from GAAP Net Income in FY2026?

In FY2026, NVIDIA generated $102,718 million in Operating Cash Flow and spent $6,042 million on Capital Expenditures, resulting in $96,676 million ($96.68 billion) in Free Cash Flow against $120,067 million in GAAP Net Income — an 80.5% Free Cash Flow conversion rate alongside $40,086 million in common stock repurchases.

Why did NVIDIA's GAAP Gross Margin compress by 390 bps in FY2026?

Per NVIDIA's FY2026 Form 10-K, GAAP Gross Margin moved from 75.0% in FY2025 ($97,858 million) to 71.1% in FY2026 ($153,463 million) as NVIDIA transitioned to full-rack Blackwell data center systems and recorded $4,000 million ($4.0 billion) in inventory and capacity write-downs (up from $1,600 million in FY2025), while net inventory expanded +112.3% YoY to $21,403 million.
