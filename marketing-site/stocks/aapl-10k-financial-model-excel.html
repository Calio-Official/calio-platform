Frequently Asked Questions (Apple 10-K & Valuation)

How do Apple's Services gross margins compare to Products gross margins?

Apple's Services segment gross margin reached 74.2%, compared to 37.1% for physical Hardware Products (iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables). High-margin recurring revenue streams from the App Store, iCloud, Apple Pay, AppleCare, and digital subscriptions have expanded Apple's consolidated gross margin from 43.3% in FY2022 to 46.2% in FY2024.

What is the scale and strategy behind Apple's share repurchase program?

Apple operates the largest capital return program in corporate history, returning over $95 billion annually through share repurchases and dividends. Apple has repurchased over $650 billion in common stock over the past decade, purposefully targeting a "net cash neutral" balance sheet while reducing diluted share count and expanding Earnings Per Share (EPS).

Why does Apple have a negative Cash Conversion Cycle?

Apple operates with a negative Cash Conversion Cycle of approximately -54 days. Due to immense scale and purchasing leverage, Apple collects cash from consumer device sales and carrier partners weeks before it pays contract manufacturing and component suppliers (Days Payable Outstanding around 110 days), effectively financing working capital via supplier credit.
