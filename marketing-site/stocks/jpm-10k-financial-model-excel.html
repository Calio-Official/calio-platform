Frequently Asked Questions (JPM 10-K & Valuation)

How did the acquisition of First Republic Bank benefit JPMorgan Chase?

JPMorgan acquired First Republic from the FDIC in May 2023, adding over $200 billion in high-net-worth wealth deposits and prime loans, contributing over $3 billion in net annual profit and accelerating private banking expansion.

What is JPMorgan's ROTCE (Return on Tangible Common Equity)?

JPMorgan delivered an industry-leading ROTCE of 22.5% in FY2024, outperforming major money-center peers (Bank of America, Citigroup, Wells Fargo) through scale advantages in credit cards, payments, and global investment banking fees.

How does JPMorgan handle credit loss provisions?

Under CECL (Current Expected Credit Losses) accounting, JPM reserves for lifetime expected losses across credit cards and commercial real estate, maintaining over $35 billion in total allowance for loan losses.
