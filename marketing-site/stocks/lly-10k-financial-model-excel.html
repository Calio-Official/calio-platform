Frequently Asked Questions (LLY 10-K & Valuation)

What is the combined market potential for Mounjaro and Zepbound (Tirzepatide)?

Tirzepatide combined revenues surpassed $16.8 billion in FY2024 and are projected by Wall Street consensus to exceed $35 billion by 2028, establishing it as one of the best-selling pharmaceutical chemical entities in history alongside semaglutide.

Why is Eli Lilly investing over $5 billion in annual capital expenditures?

To address severe global GLP-1 shortages, Lilly is building massive domestic and European fill-finish parenteral manufacturing plants (Concord NC, Lebanon IN, Alzey Germany) to ramp injectable autoinjector pen capacity.

What patent exclusivity runway does Eli Lilly have on its key drugs?

Lilly holds composition-of-matter patent protection on tirzepatide in the United States through at least 2036, providing an extended competitive moat against generic erosion.
