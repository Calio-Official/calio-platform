Frequently Asked Questions (Tesla 10-K & Valuation)

How much do Regulatory Credits contribute to Tesla's automotive gross margin?

Regulatory credits contributed $2.78 billion in FY2024 and $1.79 billion in FY2023. Because regulatory credits carry 100% gross margin with zero corresponding cost of goods sold, institutional analysts routinely strip out credits to evaluate "Automotive Gross Margin ex-Regulatory Credits" to isolate genuine manufacturing efficiency.

Why did Tesla's GAAP Net Income decline in FY2024 despite higher operating cash flow?

In FY2023, Tesla's reported Net Income ($14.997B) was elevated by a one-time non-cash tax benefit of $5.9 billion resulting from the release of valuation allowances on deferred tax assets. Normalizing for this accounting event, operating profit declined moderately from $8.89B to $7.73B due to vehicle price cuts, while Operating Cash Flow rose to $14.85B.

How fast is Tesla's Energy Generation and Storage segment expanding?

Energy Generation and Storage is Tesla's fastest growing business unit, expanding 67.7% YoY to $10.12 billion in FY2024 (up from $6.04B in FY2023 and $3.91B in FY2022). High-margin utility-scale Megapack deployments and Powerwall installations are expanding segment profitability and diversifying corporate revenue.
