Frequently Asked Questions (BRK-B 10-K & Valuation)

Why does Warren Buffett emphasize Operating Earnings over GAAP Net Income for Berkshire?

Under GAAP accounting rule ASU 2016-01, Berkshire must include unrealized market swings in its $300B+ equity portfolio (Apple, Amex, Coca-Cola) in Net Income, causing massive paper volatility that has zero bearing on underlying business performance.

How does Berkshire earn money on its $325 billion cash and Treasury bill hoard?

With 3-month US Treasury bill yields hovering near 4.5% to 5.0%, Berkshire generates over $14 billion annually in safe, short-term pre-tax interest income alone.

What is Berkshire's Insurance Float and how is it accounted for?

Insurance float ($173 billion) represents premiums collected upfront before claims are paid out. As long as underwriting breaks even or generates an underwriting profit, this float acts as cost-free permanent capital that Berkshire invests for shareholder gain.
